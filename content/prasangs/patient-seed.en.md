This is a placeholder prasang written only to test the site. It is not taken from any book or any person.

Asha planted a seed in a little pot. Then she sat down next to it and waited.

After five minutes she dug up the soil to see if it had grown. It had not. She covered it again.

After five more minutes she dug it up again. Still nothing.

Her grandmother smiled. "A seed needs water, sunlight and time," she said. "If you keep digging it up, it cannot grow."

So Asha watered the seed and went to play. Every evening she gave it a little water, and then she left it alone.

![A tall green plant with a pink flower growing in the pot.](placeholder-seedling-grown.svg)

One morning there was a green shoot. A few weeks later there was a flower.
