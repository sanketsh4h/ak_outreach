This is a placeholder prasang written only to test the site. It is not taken from any book or any person.

A student sat down one evening to study by the light of a small oil lamp. The night was warm, so he opened the window wide.

Every few minutes a gust of wind came in, and the flame bent and flickered. Each time, he stopped reading to shield it with his hand. Twice it went out, and he had to find the matches and light it again.

By midnight he had read only two pages.

An older neighbour passing by saw him struggling. "You do not need to close the window," she said. "Just close it halfway. You will still have the breeze, and the flame will stay steady."

![The same lamp burning steadily beside a half-closed window, with an open notebook nearby.](placeholder-lamp-steady.svg)

He tried it. The flame stood still, and he read until his work was done.
